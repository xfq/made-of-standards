01 · Listen to the Web

Thirty years, played as musicWeb Audio API

Every milestone is one bar of music, and the instrument changes with the era: 8-bit chiptune for the ’90s, FM bells for the 2000s, synth pads and drums for the 2010s, an ambient mix with reverb for today. No samples — every sound is synthesized live in your browser.

Drag · swipe · or click any card to hear it
